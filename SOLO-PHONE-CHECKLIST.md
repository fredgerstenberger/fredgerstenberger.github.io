# Bakchoy: solo iPhone check (about 10 minutes, one phone)

Do this on your own iPhone. Tick a box when it works. If something is wrong, write down what you tapped, what you expected and what happened.

## Before you start

- [ ] Delete any old Chowfun icon from your Home Screen. In Safari, open app.bakfan.com, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
- [ ] Open Bakchoy from the new Home Screen icon (not from Safari). It fills the screen with no Safari address bar.
- [ ] Tap More, then Settings. The version should say 100. If it is lower, wait for "Bakchoy updated · Reload" at the bottom and tap Reload.

## 1. Offline (1 minute)

- [ ] Turn on Airplane Mode. Swipe the app away and open it again. It opens right away with your recipes, plan and list.
- [ ] Turn Airplane Mode off.

## 2. Recipes (3 minutes)

- [ ] Tap a recipe in the Recipes list. The recipe slides in and the header and tab bar stay put.
- [ ] Swipe back from the left edge. The list comes back without blinking or looking like it refreshed.
- [ ] Open the recipe again, then tap the back button instead. Same result: no blink.
- [ ] On a recipe, tap the big stars to rate it. Your rating saves.
- [ ] Tap Start cooking. In Focus, swipe up for the next step and down for the one before. The step follows your finger and glides in at a pace that feels natural (not rushed).
- [ ] Tap Gather your ingredients. The panel slides in. Tick two ingredients and close it by swiping it right.
- [ ] Start a timer from a step. Its bar sits under the header on every step.
- [ ] Turn the phone on its side. Nothing is cut off. Turn it back.

## 3. Week and Today (2 minutes)

- [ ] Add a recipe to your plan from the recipe page (the + Meal plan button). Pick a day and add it.
- [ ] Swipe between Today and Week. It follows your finger.
- [ ] Remove a planned meal, then tap Undo. The meal comes back.

## 4. Grocery list (3 minutes)

- [ ] Type `ice cream` in the add box. It adds Ice cream, not cream.
- [ ] Check an item off. Tap Undo on the message. It comes back.
- [ ] Remove an item (swipe it left, tap Remove). Tap Undo. It comes back.
- [ ] Pull down from the top of the list and let go. It says "Updating…" then "Up to date".
- [ ] Tap Start shopping and leave the phone alone for 2 minutes. The screen stays on. Tap Stop shopping.
- [ ] If a "Do you have these?" question appears about something in your pantry, answer it. Nothing looks broken.

## 5. Feel (1 minute)

- [ ] Tap a tab, a chip and a row in More. Each dims the moment your finger lands.
- [ ] Open a recipe's tag box and type. The Return key says "done". Scroll the page with the keyboard up. The keyboard goes away.
- [ ] Open an empty list or recipe book (or a day with nothing planned). The bok choy shows with a next step.

## 6. Looks (1 minute)

- [ ] Turn Dark Mode on and off in Control Center. Every screen stays readable in both.
- [ ] Rotate to landscape on the grocery list. Nothing is cut off or scrolls sideways.
